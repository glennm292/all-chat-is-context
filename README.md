# all-chat-is-context

A skill for [Imbue Studio](https://imbue.com) workspaces. It installs **Context**: an app that
turns every conversation with your AI into living topics, in place of separate chat threads.

- **Topics, not threads.** Your conversations are sorted by subject, automatically, every 10
  minutes. One-offs go to *Other topics*, and what the agent did on its own goes to *Internal
  notes*.
- **Each topic shows three things:**
  - where the subject stands, in text that rewrites itself
  - the work it produced (apps running live, skills, pictures), front and centre
  - the real conversation, exactly as it happened
- **Talk in place.** Type in a topic and the conversation continues right there.
- **You own the work.** Fold the conversation away; the work stays.

## Install

Give your workspace's agent this repo's address and ask it to install the skill, or copy this
folder to `.agents/skills/all-chat-is-context/` and say "install all-chat-is-context".
`SKILL.md` has the flow. `references/design.md` has the design and how it works.
